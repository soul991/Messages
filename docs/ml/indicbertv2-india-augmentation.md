# IndicBERTv2 with Indian synthetic SMS augmentation

Updated: 2026-10-10

## Data added and safeguards

This follow-up continues the pinned `ai4bharat/IndicBERTv2-MLM-only`
experiment documented in [`indicbertv2-experiment.md`](indicbertv2-experiment.md).
The two downloaded sources are synthetic, so their performance is not treated
as evidence of real-world accuracy.

- [`scam_ham_india_14_languages`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/anmolshrivastav/scam_ham_india_14_languages)
  is MIT-licensed and tagged synthetic. The run reads only its 12,600-row
  `data/train-00000-of-00001.parquet`. It excludes 970 repeated rows (leaving
  11,630 unique rows), with no conflicting duplicate labels and no exact
  overlap with the project fit, validation, test, or frozen challenge rows.
  Neither its 1,400-row Parquet test split nor `indic_test_suite_clean.csv`
  was loaded by the trainer.
- [`indian-scam-sms-synthetic-audited`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/Ridham115/indian-scam-sms-synthetic-audited)
  is CC BY 4.0 but states its messages were generated by language models and
  the audit decisions were made by an AI assistant, not native human
  annotators. The 700 rows attributed to Groq-hosted GPT-OSS include only 110
  rows marked `keep` or `flip`; those 110 (68 scam, 42 safe) were admitted.
  The 590 unreviewed GPT-OSS rows and all 880 Gemini rows were excluded. This
  also avoids Gemini-output terms that may restrict competitor-model
  development. No added row overlaps the project splits or frozen challenge.

The training utility now enforces these filters and records source hashes,
accepted labels/language groups, invalid and duplicate rows, and protected
split overlaps. Challenge examples are loaded before augmentation; all valid
challenge text is protected from being admitted to training. Threshold
selection uses project validation ham only. Challenge results below are a
single diagnostic evaluation and did not affect training, checkpoint choice,
or threshold.

## Training and results

The pinned IndicBERTv2 revision `8598f13fe52443bc3fc054fcd665944560145b5c`
was fine-tuned on CUDA with seed `20261006`, 3 epochs, batch size 24, maximum
length 160, learning rate 2e-5, and embeddings plus the bottom 8 encoder layers
frozen. Fit size increased from 74,207 to 85,947 rows. The best checkpoint was
epoch 3 (validation loss 0.053675); the validation-ham-calibrated 1% target-FPR
threshold was `0.766085`.

| Evaluation | Recall | Safe-message FPR | Counts |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Project public test | 97.17% | 1.13% | TP 3,469 / 3,570; FP 68 / 6,030 |
| Frozen authored challenge | 33.33% | 17.78% | TP 23 / 69; FP 16 / 90 |
| Prior IndicBERTv2 baseline challenge | 33.33% | 37.78% | TP 23 / 69; FP 34 / 90 |

The augmentation reduced challenge false positives by 18 messages, but did not
recover any additional scam messages. Public-test results were already
inspected in earlier experiments and are not an unbiased estimate. The
challenge is small and authored, but its result remains a warning: the model
misses 46 of 69 challenge scams and still marks 16 safe messages. This model
is **not ready for integration or automatic filtering**. Keep the checkpoint
as an offline research artifact; continue collecting de-identified,
consented, independently human-labeled examples and separately frozen
representative evaluations before making an app decision.

The aggregate-only machine report, including checkpoint/data hashes and
metrics by source/language group, is in
[`indicbertv2-india-augmentation-report.json`](indicbertv2-india-augmentation-report.json).
The checkpoint is at `D:\Istak\Messages\indicbertv2-india-augmentation\best`
outside the Android app.
