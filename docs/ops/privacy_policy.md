# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** July 2026

At Messages, your privacy is our core design principle. We built this application to protect your communications without compromising your data. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we process, how we protect it, and your rights regarding your data.

## 1. On-Device Processing
**All message processing happens exclusively on your device.** 
- Our intelligent protection engine, which filters spam, blocks malicious content, and categorizes messages, runs 100% locally.
- We do not upload your messages, contacts, or metadata to our servers or any third-party servers.

## 2. Information We Collect
Because everything happens on-device, **we collect absolutely no personal data**.
- **Messages:** Your SMS, MMS, and RCS messages are stored securely in your device's local database and the Android Telephony Provider.
- **Contacts:** We read your contacts locally to display names and profile pictures. This data never leaves your device.
- **Analytics & Crash Reports:** We do not include any tracking SDKs, analytics, or crash reporters that phone home.

## 3. App Permissions
To function as your primary SMS app, Messages requires the following permissions:
- **SMS/MMS (Read, Send, Receive):** Required to send and receive messages.
- **Contacts (Read):** Required to match phone numbers to your saved contacts.
- **Notifications:** Required to alert you of new messages.

None of the data accessed via these permissions is transmitted over the network.

## 4. Backups (Google Drive)
If you opt-in to the Google Drive Backup feature, your encrypted backup is stored in your personal Google Drive account. We do not have access to these backups, and they are protected by Google's infrastructure.

## 5. Third-Party Integrations
We do not use any third-party services that collect your data. The pattern library used for spam filtering is updated via manual user imports or via an open-source, community-driven format that does not transmit analytics.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in this document, and the effective date will be updated accordingly.

## 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@messagesapp.example.com.
