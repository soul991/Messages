# Messages 1.6.2

This update makes the conversation list safer to use while scrolling and improves access to composing and app settings when Messages is not the default SMS app.

- Small sideways finger movement during a vertical conversation-list scroll no longer triggers archive or delete. A deliberate horizontal swipe must cross the action threshold.
- The app can open its home screen, browse conversations already available to it, and open the composer without holding the default SMS role. Sending and receiving SMS still require the app to be the default SMS app.
- Compose navigation uses the shared fade-through motion, and grouped conversation cards have a clearer continuous outline.

Validation includes the repository release checks and a synthetic 100-conversation device run. During 18 vertical scrolls with horizontal wobble, all 100 conversations remained in the inbox; a deliberate archive followed by Undo restored the synthetic row.
